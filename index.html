Before you buy

Common questions

Which nicotine strength should I choose?

If you are coming off disposables, start at 20mg nic salt — that is what most bar-style devices use. If you are refilling a bigger sub-ohm kit, 3mg to 10mg is usual, because those devices deliver far more vapour per puff. Shortfills are 0mg, so you add a nic shot to reach the strength you want.

What is the difference between nic salt and freebase?

Nic salt is smoother at high strengths, so it suits 20mg in a small pod kit. Freebase gives a stronger throat hit and is usually sold at lower strengths for larger kits. Both are nicotine — the difference is how harsh it feels, not how strong it is.

When will my order arrive?

Order before 4pm on a working day and it is dispatched the same day. Delivery is free on orders over £10.

Can I return e-liquid or pods?

Unopened items in their original sealed packaging can be returned. For hygiene and safety reasons, opened e-liquid, pods and pouches cannot be — the same rule every UK vape retailer works to.

Do I have to be 18?

Yes. It is illegal to sell nicotine products to anyone under 18 in the UK, and age is verified at checkout. Nicotine is an addictive substance.

What does TPD registered mean?

The Tobacco Products Directive sets the UK rules for nicotine e-liquid: a 10ml maximum bottle size, 20mg maximum strength, and notification to the MHRA before sale. Every nicotine line we list is notified.
